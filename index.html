Specialized Therapy Services

Therapy That Meets Your Child Where They Are

Ten specialised services, one coordinated team — each plan designed around your child's individual needs and reviewed with you as they progress.

Explore Our Therapies

Occupational Therapy Speech Therapy Physiotherapy Behaviour Therapy Special Education Sensory Integration Early Intervention Remedial Education Counselling & Parent Guidance Academic Support

Special Education

Structured learning plans designed around individual learning needs.

Lessons are broken into achievable steps, taught with multi-sensory methods and paced to the child — so concepts are truly understood, not just covered.

Occupational Therapy (OT)

Supporting fine motor skills, daily living activities, coordination and independence.

Play-based OT builds handwriting readiness, sensory processing, attention and everyday self-care skills — the foundations a child needs at school and at home.

Physiotherapy (PT)

Supporting movement, physical development, strength, posture and coordination.

Gentle, structured physical therapy helps children build strength, balance and confident movement, with exercises families can continue at home.

Speech & Language Therapy

Developing communication, language understanding, expression and speech skills.

From first words to fluent conversation, therapy works on clarity, vocabulary, comprehension and confidence in expressing needs and ideas.

Behaviour Therapy

Supporting positive behaviours, emotional regulation, social interaction and everyday skills.

With consistent routines and positive reinforcement, children learn to manage emotions, follow social cues and build habits that help them thrive.

Sensory Integration Therapy

Helping children develop sensory processing and participate more comfortably in everyday activities.

Carefully designed sensory activities help children respond to sound, touch, movement and texture more comfortably — in class, at play and at home.

Early Intervention

Providing early developmental support to help children build foundational skills.

The earlier support begins, the stronger the foundation. Early intervention nurtures communication, movement, play and learning skills in the youngest children.

Remedial Education

Providing additional academic support based on individual learning difficulties.

Targeted support for reading, writing and mathematics — including dyslexia and dysgraphia-friendly methods — that revisits gaps without pressure or stigma.

Counselling & Parent Guidance

Supporting emotional well-being and helping parents understand their child's developmental and educational needs.

Regular guidance sessions help families understand progress, practise strategies at home and feel confident partners in their child's journey.

Academic Support

Personalized educational support aligned with school curriculum and individual learning goals.

Structured coaching for Classes 6th to 12th — with a dedicated focus on 10th Std — covering concepts, homework habits and examination confidence, class by class.

Common Questions

Questions Parents Ask Us

Where exactly is Dheemahi located?

We are on the first floor above SBI Bank on Lingasugur Road, opposite Chetana Hospital, in Raichur. The map and a Get Directions button are in the Contact section below — or just call us and we will guide you.

Which classes do you support?

We support Classes 2nd to 12th — primary, middle school and secondary schooling, with structured coaching and academic support at every stage and a dedicated focus on 10th Std.

My child needs therapy more than regular classes. Can you help?

Yes. Along with special education, we offer occupational therapy, speech and language therapy, physiotherapy, behaviour therapy, sensory integration, early intervention and remedial education. If you are not sure what your child needs, talk to us first — we will listen and suggest honestly.

How do we take admission?

Fill in the enquiry form above, message us on WhatsApp, or call 99027 11588 or 99006 82340. You are also welcome to visit the academy and talk to us in person — no appointment is needed just to enquire.

What are your visiting hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 7 pm. The academy is closed on Sundays.
